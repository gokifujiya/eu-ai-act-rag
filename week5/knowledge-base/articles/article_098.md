# Article 98

## Source

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 - Artificial Intelligence Act

## Official text

Article 98 
Committee procedure
1.	
The Commission shall be assisted by a committee. That committee 
shall be a committee within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011.
2.	
Where reference is made to this paragraph, Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 
No 182/2011 shall apply.
